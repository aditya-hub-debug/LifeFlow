MYTHS VS. FACTS

Let's clear the confusion

Knowing the facts can make your first donation feel less intimidating.

MYTH

“Donating blood is very painful.”

FACT

You may feel a brief pinch when the needle is inserted.

MYTH

“I can catch an infection by donating blood.”

FACT

Donation centres use sterile, single-use equipment for each donor.

MYTH

“Only people with rare blood groups are needed.”

FACT

Every blood group is important, and supply needs change constantly.

MYTH

“I will feel weak for a long time afterward.”

FACT

Many donors return to normal activities after resting and following aftercare advice.
